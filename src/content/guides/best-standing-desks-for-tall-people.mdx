## Why Standard Standing Desks Fail Tall Users

If you are over 6 feet tall, standard dual-stage standing desks will almost certainly leave you disappointed. Most entry-level frames top out between 42 and 45 inches. When standing with proper 90-degree elbow ergonomics, a 6'2" individual requires a tabletop height of **46 to 48 inches**, while a 6'5" user needs **49 to 51 inches**.

### The 3 Golden Rules for Tall Desk Setups

1. **Opt for a 3-Stage Inverted Column**: Three telescoping segments provide the necessary stroke length to reach 50+ inches without running out of overlap between steel tubes.
2. **Prioritize Leg Column Overlap**: When a desk is fully extended, the remaining overlap inside the steel legs determines whether your monitors shake when typing. Look for columns with at least 5 inches of nested steel at maximum extension.
3. **C-Frame vs T-Frame Geometry**: C-frame legs position the lifting columns slightly behind the center of gravity, granting extra under-desk knee room and making under-desk walking pads much easier to fit.
