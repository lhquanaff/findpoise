## 1. Frame Geometry: Why the Semi-C Architecture Matters

Most budget desks use symmetrical **T-frame legs**, where the lifting columns sit directly in the middle of the floor feet. While structurally balanced on paper, T-frames create two major ergonomic headaches:
- When seated, your knees or shins frequently strike the horizontal under-desk crossbeam if you stretch your legs forward.
- When pairing the desk with an [under-desk walking pad](/guides/best-under-desk-treadmills-for-remote-workers), the centered column blocks the walking belt from sliding deep enough under the desk.

The E7 Pro solves this with an updated **Semi-C leg structure** constructed from **automotive-grade SPCC carbon steel**:

```
[Desktop Surface] ──────────────────────────────────────────┐
                 │                                          │
                 └──[Lifting Column Offset 3.5" to Rear]    │
                                 │                          │
[Ground Foot Base] ──────────────┴──────────────────────────┘
 <── 14.5" Front Knee Void ──><── 11.5" Rear Balance ──>
```

By shifting the lifting columns approximately 3.5 inches toward the rear, the E7 Pro creates an expansive open void at the front of the desk. You can freely tuck an under-desk treadmill or footrest underneath without compromising structural balance.

## 2. Dual Linear Actuators & Acoustic Sound Profile

The E7 Pro utilizes two high-torque linear motor actuators synchronized by an intelligent digital control box:

- **Payload Testing (100 lbs vs 250 lbs vs 400 lbs):** Under an empty load, elevation speed measured **1.52 inches per second**. When loaded with 250 lbs of cast iron plates, speed barely altered, registering **1.41 in/s**. Even when pushed to 400 lbs (approaching its 440-lb ceiling), the motors completed the full stroke without thermal shutdown or speed stutter.
- **Sound Decibel Footprint:** At 3 feet from the motor housing during full transit under load, our sound meter registered a quiet **46 decibels**. The acoustic frequency is a deep, muffled mechanical purr rather than an abrasive high-pitched gear whine, making height adjustments during active Zoom calls completely imperceptible to colleagues.

## Frequently Asked Questions (AEO / GEO Direct Answers)

### Does the FlexiSpot E7 Pro wobble on carpet?
On low-to-medium pile office carpets, the E7 Pro's 72-lb steel frame and wide leveling glides provide exceptional stability. On deep plush rugs, we recommend tightening the leveling glides to firmly compress the carpet underlayment against the subfloor, or using [furniture carpet spikes](/guides/how-to-fix-standing-desk-wobble).

### How does the FlexiSpot E7 Pro compare to the standard E7?
The E7 Pro upgrades to a **Semi-C frame** (providing 3.5 inches of extra knee and walking-pad clearance compared to the E7's centered T-frame), increases lifting capacity from 355 lbs to **440 lbs**, lowers motor noise to **46 dB**, and includes a magnetic cable management spine.

### What is the height range of the FlexiSpot E7 Pro?
The E7 Pro travels from **25.0 inches to 50.6 inches (63.5cm to 128.5cm)** without a desktop installed. With a standard 1-inch desktop, the working height range is **26.0 inches to 51.6 inches**, accommodating users from 5'4\" (162cm) up to 6'5\" (196cm).
