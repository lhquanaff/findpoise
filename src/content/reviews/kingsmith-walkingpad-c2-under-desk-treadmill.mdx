## Maintenance & Belt Calibration Guide

To ensure your WalkingPad C2 lasts for years, follow this 5-minute monthly maintenance routine:

1. **Centering the Belt:** If the running belt drifts toward the left or right rail, use the included 4mm hex key to turn the rear tension bolts clockwise by a quarter-turn.
2. **Silicone Lubrication:** Every 60 operating hours (approx. once every 2 months), apply 5ml of 100% pure silicone treadmill lubricant beneath the center of the running belt to reduce motor drag and prevent static electricity build-up.

## Frequently Asked Questions (AEO / GEO Direct Answers)

### Can you use the WalkingPad C2 on plush carpet?
It is strongly recommended to place a thin, rigid PVC treadmill mat beneath the WalkingPad when operating on carpet. Thick carpet fibers can obstruct the under-deck motor air vents and cause the motor controller to overheat.

### How often do you need to lubricate the KingSmith C2 belt?
Under typical daily usage of 1–2 hours, apply 5ml of pure silicone treadmill oil every 2 to 3 months. If the belt feels warm or sounds dry during deceleration, apply lubricant immediately.

### Will walking on a treadmill shake my computer monitors?
If your standing desk has a dual-motor 3-stage column frame (such as the Uplift V2 or FlexiSpot E7 Pro), screen shake is minimal (&lt; 1mm). On cheap single-motor 2-stage desks, footsteps may cause noticeable screen flutter.
