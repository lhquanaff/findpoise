## Which Desk Should You Buy?

### Buy the FlexiSpot E7 Pro if:
- You want the highest lifting capacity (440 lbs) for multi-monitor setups, heavy studio monitors, or solid hardwood tabletops.
- You want to stay under the $550 mark without sacrificing frame thickness or motor warranty.
- You prefer legroom without a lower crossbar blocking your feet or an under-desk treadmill.

### Buy the Uplift V2 if:
- You need a desk that goes exceptionally low (down to 22.6 inches for shorter users under 5'4").
- You want specialized tabletop materials like reclaimed wood, butcher block bamboo, or designer laminate.
