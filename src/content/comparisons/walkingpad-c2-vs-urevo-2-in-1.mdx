## Storage Logistics: The Make-or-Break Factor for Workstations

When adding an under-desk treadmill to your office, the biggest hurdle isn't walking—**it is what you do with the treadmill when you need to sit down in your office chair**.

---

### 1. The Sitting-to-Walking Transition Test

- **KingSmith WalkingPad C2:** You fold the back half forward over the front frame in 5 seconds. Its length shrinks to **32.5 inches**. You roll it vertically against your standing desk leg or slide it completely under your bed or credenza. Your ergonomic office chair rolls right in.
- **Urevo 2-in-1:** The frame does not fold in half. It is a full **53.1-inch rigid plank** weighing 59 lbs. Unless you have an extra 5 feet of open floor space behind your desk to push it back, you must tilt it onto tiny wheels and wrestle it into another corner of the room every time you want to sit.

**Advantage: KingSmith WalkingPad C2**

---

### 2. Motor Noise & Meeting Etiquette (Decibel Testing)

We tested both machines under identical room acoustic conditions at 1.8 mph (optimal working walking pace):

- **WalkingPad C2:** Emits **45 dB**. Its brushless motor produces a soft hum that is completely filtered out by standard Zoom or Microsoft Teams AI noise suppression.
- **Urevo 2-in-1:** Emits **51 dB**. The larger 2.25 HP motor has higher torque, but the internal cooling fan and belt roller bearings produce a more noticeable mechanical whir.

**Advantage: KingSmith WalkingPad C2**

---

### 3. Jogging Speed & Weight Limit

If you want an exercise machine that doubles as a morning cardio jogger after work:

- **Urevo 2-in-1:** Unfolds a sturdy steel handrail and ramps up to **7.6 mph**, allowing genuine 8-minute-mile running. It also supports up to **265 lbs**.
- **WalkingPad C2:** Caps out at **3.7 mph** (a fast power walk) and has a strict **220-lb capacity**. It is designed purely for active walking during desk work.

**Advantage: Urevo 2-in-1**

---

## Final Recommendation

- **Choose the KingSmith WalkingPad C2** if you want a sleek, quiet machine that fits cleanly into a modern home office, folds away effortlessly, and won't turn your desk into a cluttered gym.
- **Choose the Urevo 2-in-1** if you are on a strict $300 budget, weigh over 220 lbs, or want a single machine for both walking while typing and fast jogging after hours.
